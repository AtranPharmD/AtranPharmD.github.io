Goal: Cardiovascular & Kidney Risk Reduction*
●
+ASCVD / Indicators of High CVD Risk†
GLP-1 RA with proven CVD benefit, or SGLT2i with proven CVD benefit.
If glycemia above goal: for individuals on a GLP-1 RA, consider adding SGLT2i with proven benefit (or vice versa); pioglitazone.
●
+HF
SGLT2i with proven HF benefit in current or prior symptoms of HFrEF or HFpEF.
SGLT2i and/or either dual GIP/GLP-1 RA or GLP-1 RA with proven benefit in symptomatic HFpEF and obesity.
●
+CKD
eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73m² or albuminuria (UACR ≥30 mg/g), on max-tolerated ACEi/ARB: SGLT2i with primary evidence of reducing CKD progression (eGFR ≥20; efficacy reduced <45) or GLP-1 RA with proven CKD benefit.
If glycemia above goal: for individuals on SGLT2i, consider incorporating a GLP-1 RA (or vice versa).
If additional cardiovascular & kidney risk reduction, management of other metabolic comorbidities, and/or glycemic lowering is needed ↓
●
+Mitigating Risk of MASLD / MASH
Agents with proven or potential benefit: GLP-1 RA, dual GIP/GLP-1 RA, pioglitazone, or a combination of GLP-1 RA with pioglitazone.
Use insulin in the setting of decompensated cirrhosis.

Address Barriers to Care
  • Refer to DSMES (diabetes self-management education and support) to support self-efficacy in achieving treatment goals.
  • Consider technology (e.g., diagnostic or personal CGM) to identify therapeutic gaps and tailor therapy.
  • Identify and address social determinants of health (SDOH) that affect achievement of treatment goals.
* In people with HF, CKD, established CVD, or multiple risk factors for CVD, the decision to use a GLP-1 RA or SGLT2i with proven benefit should be made irrespective of glycemic goal attainment.
† ASCVD: established CVD (e.g., MI, stroke, arterial revascularization). Indicators of high risk: most definitions comprise ≥55 years of age with two or more additional risk factors (obesity, hypertension, smoking, dyslipidemia, or albuminuria).
‡ Low-dose pioglitazone may be better tolerated and similarly effective as higher doses.

Converting Between Insulins

Most insulin conversions are 1:1 (the dose of the new insulin is usually the same as the old insulin). There are two named exceptions below. The new insulin dose may still need adjustment if BG is not controlled.

Converted Dose
—
Example (NPH→glargine): NPH 30 units AC breakfast + 20 units AC dinner = 50 units NPH/day → 50 × 0.8 = 40 units glargine once daily.

Liraglutide (Victoza; Saxenda for weight loss)0.6 mg SC daily ×1wk, ↑ to 1.2 mg daily. Saxenda max 1.8 mg daily.Boxed warning: thyroid C-cell tumors (all except Byetta). ↓A1C 0.5–1.5%. Low hypoglycemia risk unless with insulin.
Dulaglutide (Trulicity)0.75 mg SC weekly; max 4.5 mg weekly.CVD benefit. Cardiorenal-preferred agent.
Semaglutide (Ozempic SC; Rybelsus oral; Wegovy for weight loss)SC: 0.25 mg weekly ×4wk → 0.5 mg weekly, max 2 mg weekly. PO: 3 mg daily ×30 days → 7 mg daily, max 14 mg daily.CVD benefit (SC). Rybelsus: take ≥30 min before first food/drink.
Exenatide (Byetta); Exenatide ER (Bydureon BCise)Byetta: 5 mcg SC BID, max 10 mcg BID. ER: 2 mg SC weekly.Byetta: give ≤60 min before meals. Exenatide is on the NIOSH hazardous drug list. ER not recommended if eGFR < 45.
Tirzepatide (Mounjaro; Zepbound for weight loss) — dual GLP-1/GIP2.5 mg SC weekly ×4wk, ↑ to 5 mg weekly; max 15 mg weekly.Supported for HFpEF (guideline-based, SUMMIT trial). Highest weight-loss efficacy in class.
Class boxed warning (except Byetta): risk of thyroid C-cell carcinoma — avoid with personal/family history of MTC or MEN2. Class warnings: pancreatitis, acute kidney injury, gallbladder disease. All are SC injections except oral semaglutide.

Drug ClassNotes
Alpha-Glucosidase Inhibitors (Acarbose, Miglitol)Taken with first bite of each meal. Do not cause hypoglycemia alone, but if hypoglycemia occurs from another drug, must treat with glucose tablets/gel (NOT sucrose/table sugar/juice — these won't work).
Bile Acid Binding Resin (Colesevelam / Welchol)Also indicated for dyslipidemia. Constipation most common side effect; can bind and ↓ absorption of other drugs.
Dopamine Agonist (Bromocriptine)Contraindicated with syncopal migraines and in postpartum/breastfeeding (inhibits lactation).
Amylin Analog (Pramlintide / Symlin, SC injection)Used in T1D or T2D with mealtime insulin. Must reduce mealtime insulin dose by 50% when starting (significant hypoglycemia risk). Contraindicated with gastroparesis.
